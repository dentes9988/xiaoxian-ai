# Journal Target Memo for `xiaoxian AI`

Date: 2026-06-22

## Recommended Submission Strategy

My recommendation is to use a two-track strategy:

1. **Submit now to a concept-friendly journal** with the current manuscript framed as a human-centered AI architecture and design position paper.
2. **Prepare a stronger second paper later** after collecting real usage data, local fine-tuning comparisons, and longitudinal evidence.

## Best Fit Right Now

### 1. AI & SOCIETY

Best current fit for the manuscript in its present form.

Why it fits:

- the journal focuses on societal, cultural, cognitive, ethical, and philosophical implications of digital systems
- it is friendly to work that discusses how digital systems should be conceived, used, and regulated
- your paper’s strongest contribution is not only technical, but socio-technical: user sovereignty, private adaptation, and a less extractive human-AI relationship

Why I rank it first:

- the paper currently reads more like a design argument with a working artifact than a benchmark-heavy systems paper
- the journal scope is broad enough to support a serious conceptual contribution grounded in implementation

Official sources:

- Overview: [AI & SOCIETY](https://link.springer.com/journal/146)
- Submission guidelines: [AI & SOCIETY submission guidelines](https://link.springer.com/journal/146/submission-guidelines)

Suggested framing:

- human-centered personal AI
- privacy-preserving personalization
- user-sovereign self-modeling
- open-source design case for future AI ecosystems

## Strong Secondary Options

### 2. Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence

Good option if you want an explicitly AI-facing journal and are comfortable with an open-access submission route.

Why it fits:

- the journal is multidisciplinary and explicitly welcomes work bringing AI into practical use
- its scope includes human learning and behavior change, AI in business, and broader applied AI questions
- article types are flexible, which can be useful if we choose a perspective, conceptual analysis, or technology-oriented format

Official sources:

- About: [Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/artificial-intelligence/about)
- Article types: [Frontiers article types](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/artificial-intelligence/for-authors/article-types)
- Author guidelines: [Frontiers author guidelines](https://www.frontiersin.org/guidelines/author-guidelines)

Best use case:

- if you want the paper to sit more clearly in applied AI rather than social theory

### 3. Personal and Ubiquitous Computing

Good fit after one more iteration, especially if we strengthen the paper with a user study or a field deployment section.

Why it fits:

- the journal focuses on human-centered research on personal and ubiquitous technologies and services
- the system is literally a personal, daily-use, local-first companion technology

Why it is not my first choice today:

- the current draft needs more evaluation to fully match what readers there may expect from a strong ubicomp-style systems paper

Official sources:

- Aims and scope: [Personal and Ubiquitous Computing aims and scope](https://link.springer.com/journal/779/aims-and-scope)
- How to publish: [Personal and Ubiquitous Computing publishing options](https://link.springer.com/journal/779/how-to-publish-with-us)
- Submission guidelines: [Personal and Ubiquitous Computing submission guidelines](https://link.springer.com/journal/779/submission-guidelines)

## Better After More Evidence

### 4. ACM Transactions on Interactive Intelligent Systems

This is a serious and relevant target, but I would not make it the first submission with the current manuscript.

Why it fits eventually:

- the journal explicitly covers interactive intelligent systems
- your work is about a concrete interactive intelligent system architecture

Why I would wait:

- TIIS is a better target after we add a stronger evaluation section, clear system comparisons, and perhaps a user study

Official sources:

- Journal home: [ACM TiiS](https://dl.acm.org/journal/tiis)
- Author guidelines: [ACM TiiS author guidelines](https://dl.acm.org/journal/tiis/author-guidelines)

## Recommendation Summary

If you want to move fast, I recommend:

1. revise the current draft for **AI & SOCIETY**
2. prepare a tailored cover letter
3. optionally prepare a second version for **Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence**
4. later run a user study and comparison experiments, then target **Personal and Ubiquitous Computing** or **ACM TiiS**

## What We Still Need Before Actual Submission

To make the paper more convincing, I recommend adding:

1. one architecture diagram
2. two or three screenshots from the product
3. a short method section describing the prototype implementation
4. one small internal evaluation or design walkthrough
5. a sharper limitations section explicitly distinguishing the current system from therapy, diagnosis, or deterministic personality prediction
