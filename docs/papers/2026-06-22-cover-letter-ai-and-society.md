Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, **“Local Self-Training, Cloud Execution: A User-Sovereign Architecture for Personal AI Companions,”** for publication in *AI & SOCIETY*.

This paper presents a human-centered architecture for personal AI assistants that addresses a growing tension in contemporary AI systems: stronger personalization often requires increasingly intimate user data, yet centralizing that data undermines privacy, user sovereignty, and trust. We propose a split architecture in which a stronger online model handles live conversation, instruction return, structured extraction, and tool orchestration, while a smaller local model supports long-term private personalization through local organization and periodic fine-tuning.

The manuscript’s contribution is both technical and socio-technical. In addition to describing an open-source prototype, `xiaoxian AI`, the paper argues for a different relationship between people and AI systems: one in which personal continuity is not treated as a platform-owned inference layer, but as a locally governed user asset. The work also introduces a time-aware and revisable self-model, explicit confirmation for high-impact profile changes, and a low-authority prior translation framework intended to support understanding without deterministic labeling.

We believe the manuscript fits *AI & SOCIETY* because its primary contribution lies at the intersection of AI design, human values, privacy, identity, and the social shaping of digital systems. The paper is not framed as a pure benchmark study; rather, it is a grounded design proposal for a more human-centered and less extractive form of personal AI.

This manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and all authors have approved its submission.
